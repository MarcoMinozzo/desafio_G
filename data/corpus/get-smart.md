Get Smart is a Getnet card machine exclusive to companies with monthly billing above BRL 10,000. The product page states a free account and card, receipt in 2 days, and a free-machine condition tied to sales from BRL 60,000. That threshold is higher than the Get Classica free-machine condition.

The page adds GetStore, an app store, a kiosk mode for self-service, a barcode reader, a camera, an invoice printer, and split payment. The Get Smart manual describes a touchscreen, chip reader, magnetic stripe, integrated NFC contactless reader, and connectivity by 4G, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth. The box includes a dual-voltage charger and a 4G chip with a data plan.

Pix on Get Smart works by entering the amount, confirming, and choosing Pix. The customer pays by QR code or NFC tap. Pix receipt must be enabled in the Getnet Brazil app before that sale mode is used. Published rates on the product page are promotional and the page says they change with the contract.

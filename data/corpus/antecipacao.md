Receivables advance (antecipacao) lets a Getnet merchant receive credit-card sales sooner. The help page covers credit sales, either in a single payment or in installments. It is done in the Getnet Brazil app on Android or iOS.

The published path is: sign in, open Services, choose Sales advance, review the amount available to advance and the registered receiving accounts, pick the account that should receive the money, accept the terms, simulate the advance, check amounts, card brands, and fees, confirm, review the final amount, choose Advance, and enter the password. The minimum amount on that page is BRL 50. Status is followed under My Orders.

The Get Smart manual also describes antecipacao on the terminal: Payment, Advance, merchant password, choose the brand, consult, then Advance with the code shown in the consultation. A receipt is printed. The same manual says the app or the My Account portal can enable a one-off or an automatic advance. The fee is part of the simulation, not a single number published as permanent on the help page.

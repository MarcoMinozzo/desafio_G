---
title: Crediario installments
url: https://site.getnet.com.br/crediario/
---

Crediario is a Getnet credit option that lets the cardholder split a purchase. The product page says the merchant may offer installment plans of up to 48 times. The same page says the maximum term and the interest rate vary by the card issuer, so 48 is a ceiling, not a number every card will allow.

The merchant simulates the plan on the machine before charging, so the customer sees installments and charges. The merchant receives the sale in up to 2 business days, in one payment, regardless of how many installments the cardholder chose. The page describes a single discount fee and no separate cost to bring the receipt forward for this mode. The exact discount rate is in the contract and is viewed in the Getnet Brazil app or the customer portal, not as one public number on the page.

Enablement is requested in the app or through the call center and is subject to Getnet review. On the machine the path is credit, then crediario, then the installment count, then a simulation the cardholder can accept. If the option is missing, the FAQ points to a normal card sale or to terminal initialization under Support, Card brands, Technical functions, Initialization.

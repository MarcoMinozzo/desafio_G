Get Classica is a Getnet card machine offered to individuals and companies. The product page states a free account and card together with the machine, and receipt in 2 days. A free machine is tied to sales from BRL 10,000. On the same page, a monthly-sales comparison places Get Classica in the BRL 10,000 band and Get Smart in the BRL 60,000 band.

The published card rates on that page are promotional. The terms say those rates change with the contracted products and that existing customers must simulate the rate in the Getnet app. Prepaid credit is described as received in up to 2 business days and prepaid debit in up to 1 business day.

The Get Classica manual describes payment by magnetic stripe, chip, and tap (NFC), including phones and watches. Pix is available on the machine: enter the amount, confirm, and choose Pix, then the customer scans a QR code or taps with NFC.

---
title: Payment Link and WhatsApp
url: https://site.getnet.com.br/link-de-pagamento/
---

The Getnet Payment Link is for selling without a physical checkout. The product page says the merchant can sell through WhatsApp or a social network by sharing the link. The buyer can pay with debit, credit, Pix, a digital wallet, or a bank slip.

The link is created in the My Account portal or in the Getnet Brazil app. The published steps are: sign in, open Products and Services, select Payment Link, create the link, fill in the product, shipping, and payment options, then copy or share it. The page also describes fraud protection, a product image, more than one product in the same cart, and an optional shipping amount.

The page lists plan prices and says those fees change with the contracted products. A merchant should confirm the current price in the app or on the page rather than rely on a remembered figure. No online store is required to send the link.

The Get Smart manual lists Wi-Fi, 4G, and Bluetooth on the terminal, and a status icon for each. The box includes a 4G chip with a data plan. The manual tells the merchant to use the original charger and not to open the terminal; only an authorized technician may open it.

When a payment mode is missing, the crediario FAQ tells the merchant to run a normal card sale or to initialize the terminal under Support, Card brands, Technical functions, Initialization. The public Getnet pages publish support at 4002-4000 and 4003-4000 for capitals and metropolitan areas, and 0800-648-8000 for other locations, from 06:00 to 22:00.

The manuals describe how a sale is approved and printed. They do not publish a catalogue of decline codes. A decline on a specific merchant's machine has to be read from that merchant's terminal record and then escalated to Getnet support with the serial number if retries fail.

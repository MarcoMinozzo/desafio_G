The global Getnet site describes a payments company operating in Latin America and Europe, including Brazil. It presents in-store, online, and on-the-go acceptance, an omnichannel connection between physical and digital sales, and financial tools aimed at flexibility. Product rules for the Brazilian market are published on the Brazilian Getnet pages, not on this global overview.

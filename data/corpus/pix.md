---
title: Pix and the receiving account
url: https://site.getnet.com.br/pagamentos-digitais/
---

To receive sales via Pix, the merchant enables Pix in the Getnet Brazil app. The public pages do not require a pre-existing third-party bank account before that switch is turned on. They do say the money is paid into a registered receiving account, and the machine offer includes a free Getnet account and card for a merchant who does not already have one. Registering a bank account for receipt is a task in the app and in the My Account portal, alongside enabling Pix.

Getnet integrates Pix with the Get Classica and Get Smart machines. The merchant enters the amount, confirms, and selects Pix. The customer pays by QR code or by tap (NFC). The digital-payments page says a Pix amount can reach the account in seconds, and that the merchant can reconcile Pix together with other receipts.

The Get Smart manual says Pix receipt has to be enabled in the Getnet Brazil app. The same manual lists, among the app and My Account portal tasks, enabling Pix receipt and registering bank accounts used for receipt. The machine product pages offer a free Getnet account and card together with the terminal.
